Welcome to Peek

Preview any link without leaving the page, and get a quick summary when you hover one. Here's everything you need.

1 Peek at a link

  • Hold Shift and click a link, or right-click it and choose Peek link.
  • Esc closes the peek. ⌘O opens it in a new tab.
  • Dragging a link never triggers a peek.
  • Instagram and X open in a new tab instead, because they don't work inside a peek. You can change that list in the toolbar popup.

2 Summarize a link

  • On search pages, just hover a link for a moment.
  • On any other site, hold Shift while hovering.

3 Choose where summaries come from

Summaries are written by an AI model that runs on your computer, so your browsing stays private. Peek needs one of these:

Checking for a model on this computer…
  • Apple Intelligence (Mac): install the free apfel helper. Setup steps
  • Ollama or LM Studio, if you already use one. Ollama steps · LM Studio steps
  • Chrome's built-in model: Chrome 138 or newer. Open Peek's toolbar popup and click Enable summaries.

Then click the Peek icon in your toolbar to pick your engine, server address and model. If you don't see the icon, pin it from the puzzle-piece menu. Full instructions

You can reopen this page any time from the toolbar popup.
